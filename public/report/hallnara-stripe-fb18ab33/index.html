Hallnara Center, payment test

Stripe payment test report

25 November 2026. Every payment ran in Stripe test mode with test cards, so no real money moved.

Summary

  • A real Stripe test payment went through from start to finish: approval, invoice email, payment page, card payment, paid, ready to ship.
  • We then simulated 10 customers paying within the same minute and ran it 4 times. Every order was charged once, stock stayed exact and each order got one shipment. Stripe returned no errors.
  • The busy minute exposed 6 weak spots in our own system. All 6 are fixed and covered by automatic tests.
  • Still to test once accounts are ready: the live key, Stripe's real payment notification (needs the Cloudflare account), Zoho Books and Aramex (waiting for credentials).

Test 2

The busiest minute, run 4 times

In one minute: 10 customers order and get approved together, each opens the payment link twice, Stripe sends every payment notice twice, one customer pays a second time from an old browser tab, and the mailbox refuses mail above 30 a minute, the way Microsoft 365 does.
